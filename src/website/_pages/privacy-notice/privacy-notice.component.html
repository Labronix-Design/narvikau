Privacy & Data.

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Enquiry Data

When you request information about a Navrik canopy, we collect the details needed to respond and recommend a suitable product.

  • Canopy guidance: We use your name, contact details, vehicle information and message to understand your requirements.
  • Communication: We use your email address or phone number to respond to your enquiry and provide support.

2. Protecting Information

We limit access to personal information and use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we hold.

  • Service providers: Information may be processed by providers that help us host the site, deliver messages, measure consented analytics or support customers.
  • No data sales: Navrik does not sell your personal information to data brokers.

3. Local Storage & Analytics

We use essential browser storage to remember privacy choices and support reliable site operation.

Only when you choose to allow analytics do we use aggregated website measurement to understand traffic and improve the site. You can decline without affecting canopy enquiries or support.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to ask about the personal information we hold, request correction or raise a privacy concern. Applicable rights and exceptions are governed by Australian privacy law.
